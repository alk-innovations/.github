# ALK Innovations

ALK Innovations is the entrepreneurial holding and laboratory of Quentin Georget, based in France.
It builds simple, affordable software for the people big platforms are too expensive or too
complicated for: associations, schools, families and small teams.

Each product is designed, built and run as its own autonomous project, with its own site,
its own code base and its own infrastructure. What they share is a way of working: plain
language, honest pricing, privacy by default, and tools that do one job well.

## Products

### [Trupeo](https://www.trupeo.com): the shared mailbox for small teams

Trupeo turns one email address (contact@, info@, secretariat@...) into a shared inbox the
whole team works from, without a help desk and without changing address.

- Works with **Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and any IMAP mailbox**.
- See who is handling each email, assign it to someone, and stop answering the same mail twice.
- Leave notes for your teammates next to the conversation, organise mail with labels and scopes.
- Built for associations, schools, small businesses, co-ownership and HOA boards.
  Verified associations and schools get a reduced rate.
- Available in English and French, with a [Claude plugin](https://github.com/alk-innovations/trupeo-claude-plugin)
  to read and answer the shared inbox from an AI assistant.

### [Crenomi](https://www.crenomi.com): every calendar, one availability

Crenomi keeps a "Busy" blocker in each of your calendars, so every calendar shows when you
are really free. Work, personal and client calendars stay in sync, and anyone can book time
with you without double bookings.

### [Zibou](https://www.zibou.app): maths and French as games, from CP to CM2

Zibou follows the French national curriculum (Éducation nationale) class by class, from CP to
CM2: short, playful mini-games in maths and French, sorted by school year. Free, no account,
no advertising.
